01 — Explore

A Living Map of Biblical History

Pinch to zoom, swipe to pan. The complete genealogy of Scripture spreads out before you — thousands of years of lineage, connected and explorable for the first time on your phone.

02 — Search

Find Anyone in Scripture, Instantly

Search by name and Patria navigates you directly to their place in the tree. Abraham, David, Rahab, Methuselah — see exactly where they fit in the grand story.

03 — Discover

The Story Behind Every Name

Tap any person to open their biography — key details, historical context, and their role in the arc of Scripture. Every name in the genealogy has a story worth knowing.

04 — Study

Scripture at Your Fingertips

Key Bible references accompany every biography. Let the genealogies of Genesis, Chronicles, and the Gospels come alive with context you can see and understand.

From Abraham and David to Methuselah and the sons of Shem — every person in the tree has a biography. 969 years of life. One tap away.
